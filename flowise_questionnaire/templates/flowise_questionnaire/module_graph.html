How to read this graph

  • Numbered blue circles are questionnaire questions in extracted module order.
  • The green numbered circle is the first question detected inside this module.
  • This does not always mean an unconditional start; the question may still depend on external prerequisites.
  • Dashed grey arrows labelled next mean normal sequence flow.
  • Green conditional arrows are verified as covered by deterministic coverage intents.
  • Amber conditional arrows are unmatched/unknown coverage and need inspection.
  • Orange boxes and arrows represent loop containers or repeated groups.
  • Click a node or edge to see a readable routing explanation.
  • Clicking a node highlights that question, its prerequisite/source nodes, and incoming dependency routes.
